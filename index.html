Our Story
Made for BUETIANS.
Worn with Pride.

BUET Tees was started by two BUET students — Ashik and Kawsar — who wanted to make quality, well-designed t-shirts specifically for the BUET community. No generic prints. No compromise on fabric. Just clean designs that actually represent what it means to study at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology.

We handle everything — design, sourcing, printing, and delivery — right to your hall room. Campus delivery is always free, and we deliver within 6 days. Every order is printed on-demand, so you're getting a fresh shirt made just for you.
